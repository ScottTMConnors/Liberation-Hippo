# 1.1.32

## 🎮 Zeus for every player
- Every player now has a limited Zeus that shows the area within 500 m of you and your units. Nothing can be spawned, moved, edited or deleted from it.
- Double-click your own squad, high-command groups or vehicles to remote control them; press the Zeus key to return.
- The build menu now places vehicles, defences and buildings through Zeus: left-click places, R / Shift+R rotates, PgUp / PgDn changes height, Esc cancels.
- Admins get a Master Zeus button in the admin menu for the full game master; closing it returns them to the limited Zeus.

## 🎖️ Ranks
- New 18-rank US Army ladder from Private to General, each with its own insignia.
- A rank strip on the HUD shows your insignia and XP progress to the next rank.
- Promotions and demotions show a card with your new badge and what it unlocks.
- Your character's in-game rank and nametag now follow your mission rank.

## 🖥️ Redesigned interface
- Every menu has a new, consistent look: redeploy, build, settings, squad, admin, shops, garage, warehouse, AI command, Mag Repack and more.
- The redeploy screen has a docked panel with a minimap and orbiting camera, instant selection and shorter transitions.
- A compact HUD card, with alerts and news shown as cards in the top right instead of text in the middle of the screen.
- Joining is faster, and Esc opens the pause menu on the start and first deploy screens.
- New 13-chapter tutorial. Any key closes it, and it no longer locks your controls.
- Map pages rebuilt: About, Links (Discord and GitHub), Credits, readable Settings, and a Field Guide with ranks, XP rewards, civilian reputation, shortcuts, aircraft loadouts and factions.
- Difficulty levels now have hippo names, with the real level in brackets.

## 🚗 Civilians and traffic
- Ambient civilians only appear in towns, away from military sectors and FOBs. FOBs and bases no longer get ambient staff, so no more mixed-side groups.
- Civilian cars now drive between nearby towns and are cleared away when no players are near.
- Air traffic is rare: at most one aircraft, two at high density.

## 🔧 Fixes
- Older or partly damaged saves now load instead of failing.
- Recruit, build and recycle stats now count reliably.
- The server blocks more faked commands from clients, such as ammo, vehicle locks, mobile respawns, prisoners and truck loading.
