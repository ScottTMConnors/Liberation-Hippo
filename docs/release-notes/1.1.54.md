# 1.1.54

## 🪖 New: Fireteams
- New Fireteams action opens a map window listing your squadmates grouped by vehicle.
- Split selected soldiers into fireteams, on foot or with their whole vehicle, and command them from the map.
- A message tells you when one of your fireteams is wiped out.

## 🗺️ Map
- Every player's fireteams, squads and vehicles are shown: yours green, other players' purple, AI forces blue.
- Hover a marker for its owner, size and vehicle.

## 💣 Minefields
- Roads into a sector, and open ground around bases and radio towers, can be mined when its battle starts.
- Mines are cleared once the sector is captured.
- The Minefields setting sets the chance of each approach being mined (25% by default).

## 🚙 AI vehicle crews
- Crews start in a free MRAP and earn points from kills.
- When their vehicle is lost they buy a tank, APC or MRAP depending on their points.
- They spawn well clear of FOBs and repair their vehicle in the field.

## 👥 Squad
- Squadmates keep their vehicle seats when you go down or die and an AI takes the lead.
- Medics only come when called, and a medic treating someone is no longer pulled back into a seat.

## 📦 Garage and cargo
- Garage and Cargo work anywhere inside a FOB's area.
- The cargo manager has a Garage button.

## ⚙️ Settings
- Settings are regrouped into clear sections with rewritten names and descriptions.
- Removed settings that no longer did anything.

## 🛠️ Fixes
- The server is ready in about 4 seconds instead of 24.
- No more camera flashes between the intro, deploy screen and spawn.
- Scores are no longer lost when several players' scores update at once.
- Fixed a Zeus hand-over that could fail to give Master Zeus.
- Fixed a HUD error right after joining.
