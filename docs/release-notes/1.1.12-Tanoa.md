# 1.1.12

## 🦛 Now Hippos Conquest
- Tanoa now runs the Hippos Conquest version of the mission, the same as Malden.

## ⚔️ AI Warfare
- Configurable AI Warfare: friendly and enemy AI battlegroups fight over sectors on their own.
- Easy AI and battle scope settings to tune how hard and how large the fighting gets.
- Extra combat options in the mission settings.
- AI Warfare, FOB defenders and vehicle crews spread across headless clients, so busy servers stay smoother.

## 🛒 Catalog
- Optional searchable catalog of every loaded faction and vehicle, with filters for unlocked items, counts and clear purchase details.

## 🔄 A year of Liberation RX updates
- AI logistics convoys by truck and helicopter.
- Flatbed transport for big objects, repair stations and off-road repair.
- New eject and onboard actions, extra squads and dynamic player actions.
- Prisoners behave properly in buildings and vehicles and no longer escape or redeploy wrongly.
- Build trenches, large storage and building ownership.
- Undercover setting, protection on a second chance, semi-unconscious state and saved player kill stats.
- Better ACE support (repair, carrying, FOB building, respawn) and saved TFAR settings.
- Support for SPE, CSLA and CFP factions, NATO PMC as an enemy and the MIM-145 Defender.
- Many fixes to building, markers, side missions, saves, the arsenal and multiplayer actions.

## 🎯 Sectors and objectives
- Sector commanders now have an outpost with guards and nearby garrisons, and eliminating one always counts.
- Commanders no longer spawn in awkward spots on piers and the coast.
- Defended sectors are better populated.
- Redesigned objective HUD that tracks several objectives at once.

## 🏗️ FOBs
- Fortification builds are much faster and play a single construction sound.

## 🧍 Civilians
- Civilians move around more naturally and react better to damage.
- Removed the civilian and friendly-fire punishments.
- Removed the news broadcasts.

## ⚔️ AI and rewards
- Enemy vehicles retry their deployment instead of never arriving.
- More AI runs on headless clients, easing server load.
- Kills are credited consistently, including vehicles that die after you damage them and kills made by your squad.

## ⚙️ Settings
- The Friendly-fire enforcement and Civilian casualty penalty settings are back, both off by default.
- The objective panel now fits ultrawide screens.

## 🌐 Multiplayer
- AI groups run by headless clients now follow battlegroup orders correctly.
- R3F heliport drops and rappelling work properly in multiplayer.
- Commander and moderator lists now come from the server, so a server's own lists reach every player.

## 🔧 Fixes
- Deleting a sector commander now counts as eliminating it.
- Manned trench guns are no longer cleaned up.
- Rejoining during a respawn cooldown no longer kicks you.
- Call Medic keeps the replacement medic, and the revive menu reopens after Esc.
- Recruited units get their proper loadouts, and the sell shop sells the right items.
- Side missions and wildlife keep running on an empty server.
- Many smaller script errors fixed after a full review.
