# 1.1.52

## 📦 New: Cargo and loot
- New Cargo action at every FOB: your home stockpile, garage and nearby vehicles and crates in one screen.
- Your own home stockpile: unlimited storage for weapons, magazines, items, crates and boxes, saved with your profile.
- Move a stack, one item or everything between home, stored vehicles and nearby vehicles.
- Unload all vehicles sends every vehicle's cargo home in one click.
- Take items out into a crate next to you, or place a stored crate or vehicle like a build.
- Send cargo to another player's home.
- Sell anything for 75% of its value. Scrap a stored vehicle for 75% of its build cost, less its damage.
- Loot unclaimed vehicles and crates, captured enemy ones included. Stolen enemy vehicles and gear are worth money.

## 🚗 Garage
- Stored vehicles keep their fuel, ammo, damage, camo and parts like slat cages and camo nets.
- Their inventory and cargo are kept too, even a carried quad bike with its own load.
- The garage list shows fuel, ammo and damage for every vehicle.
- New Service button: repair, rearm and refuel a stored vehicle for the usual price.
- New Cargo button: open a stored vehicle's cargo.
- Vehicles no longer need a repair before you store them.
- Vehicle ammo now reads correctly. Before, a vehicle showed full until its last magazine was empty.

## 💾 Saving
- Your vehicles go into your garage on every save, wherever they are and even while driving. After a restart they wait in the garage.
- Items in your crates go to your home stockpile on every save, so a restart loses nothing.
- A sector under attack during a save stays yours.
- Autosave now runs every 15 minutes by default.
- Saves from a newer mission version are refused instead of loading wrongly.

## 👥 Squad
- The crew of a vehicle you build are normal squad members: saved with your squad and redeployed around you.
- Vehicle crews rejoin your squad after you reconnect.

## ❤️ Revive
- Huge hits (destroyed vehicles, big explosions, shells) now kill outright instead of leaving you wounded.
- If you die outright, you respawn with the loadout you died with (paid in ammo like a respawn loadout).

## 🛠️ Build menu
- Sort by clicking the column headers.
- Each filter has its own reset; the faction filter starts on the mission's faction.
- The list no longer flickers or jumps while it refreshes.

## 🗳️ Commander vote
- Clicking a sector on the vote map picks the sector the hover ring shows.

## 📖 Help
- New tutorial page and Field Guide page on cargo, loot and the garage.
