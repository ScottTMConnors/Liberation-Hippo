# 1.1.21

## 🗺️ Command AI forces
- The commander can order assault squads and armor to attack a sector, defend a sector or defend a FOB from "Command AI forces" on the map or action menu.
- Each group has an AUTO option for what it does after its target is captured. FOB guards always stay at their FOB.

## 📡 Living map
- Friendly AI groups appear with NATO symbols, objective lines, combat rings and live tracers.
- Enemy groups show only while your side knows about them, with a "?" at their last known position.
- Shots from distant firefights now reach the map.
- New Friendly Forces / INTEL panel with layer toggles.
- The map shows vote totals per sector and highlights your own vote in green.

## 🏕️ Sectors and bases
- FOBs and military bases are staffed by faction soldiers instead of civilians; towns keep civilians.
- Headless clients now run sector building garrisons and wall guards.
- Merged upstream Liberation RX: team-kill punishment rework, personal box fix and more enemy patrols (up to 12).
- The AI load menu shows your FPS and ping, ping per headless client and a breakdown of unmarked AI.

## 💰 Economy
- Failed direct builds no longer charge you or delete vehicles.
- Garage store/unload no longer duplicates cargo, warehouse boxes can't be paid twice, and nothing can be bought without paying.
- The sell shop re-prices when you confirm.

## 🔧 Fixes
- Reset progress no longer stops with "A saved faction is unavailable"; it falls back to the default factions.
- Side missions no longer lock for the whole session; Baron Rouge, Town Invasion, Special Delivery, SAR and FOB hunting outcomes fixed.
- Scoreboard kills no longer double on respawn, and score, ammo, garage and arsenal carry over on every side.
- Players still loading are no longer treated as disconnected; their arsenal box and taxi are removed when they leave.
- Undercover detection and enemy patrols work with headless clients.
- Ambient soldiers and restored vehicle crews stay on their own side.
- Medics are re-sent for wounded players, and AI logistics no longer loses boxes or keeps driving after Continue.
- Admin and idle kicks now actually kick.
- Revive kit and role rules apply in modes 2 and 3, and wounded players no longer bleed out while carried in vehicles.
- R3F towing and locking, HALO drops, redeploy loadouts, airdrop costs, player markers and FOB actions after respawn fixed.
- The intro no longer shows your character before the camera starts.
- "Log Mission Settings" now works; unused Permissions and civilian roster settings removed.

## 🌐 Performance and servers
- Much less periodic network traffic, and less per-frame work in the HUD.
- The server checks more commands sent by clients.
- Auto-started headless clients now get the server's join password.

## 🧱 Town walls
- Active towns are now walled. Concrete walls follow the town edge, and one demo charge knocks a section down.
- Roads through the wall get a checkpoint with a boom gate, guard tower, bunker and guards.
- Manned guard towers stand along the wall.
- Turn it off with the new "Town walls" setting.
