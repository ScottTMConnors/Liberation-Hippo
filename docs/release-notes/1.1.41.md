# 1.1.41

## 🎖️ Ranks and progression
- New XP curve: early ranks come quickly, later ranks take longer.
- Every rank from Private First Class to Colonel now unlocks new units, vehicles or support.
- Many vehicles and units unlock earlier, ranked by their armor and firepower.
- Squad presets now unlock from Second Lieutenant to Major instead of Colonel only.
- Your saved XP moves to the new curve, so you keep your rank.
- The Field Guide rank page lists what each rank unlocks.

## 🛠️ Build menu
- Anyone can now build a vehicle with its crew, not just the commander.
- The crew joins your own squad.
- The crew option now shows clearly as ON or OFF and reads "Spawn with crew".
- Locked items name the exact rank you need.

## 👥 Lobby
- The Commander is now the first slot.

## 🧹 Cleanup
- Removed the "A lucky day...", "lucky boy" and "no radio tower nearby" chat messages.
