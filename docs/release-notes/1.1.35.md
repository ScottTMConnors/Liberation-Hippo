# 1.1.35

## 🛠️ Build menu
- The menu is bigger, with a taller item list and slightly smaller rows, so you see far more items at once.
- Every other row has a faint band to make long lists easier to read.
- Locked items show a small red lock next to their greyed-out name instead of "[Locked]".
- Selecting a vehicle shows its armor, engine power, top speed, crew and passenger seats, fuel tank, inventory space, weapons and logistics (cargo capacity, towing, sling-lifting).
- A single cost table shows what the item costs next to what you have; anything you can't afford is red.
- "Build with crew" is now a checkbox. Unticked costs 0 manpower; ticked shows the crew's manpower cost.
- The menu no longer rebuilds itself a second time right after opening.

## 👥 Manpower
- Vehicles only use manpower while they have a living AI crew. Uncrewed vehicles, mobile respawns and captured vehicles no longer take manpower.

## 🏕️ FOB actions
- Build and Virtual Garage no longer show up twice in the action menu after respawning.

## 🛡️ Admin
- The admin menu has a Mission admin button in its Campaign section.
- The separate Mission admin and AI load actions are gone from the scroll menu; both are in the admin menu.
