# 1.1.33

## 🩹 Wounded camera
- When you go down, the camera now lifts out of your eyes and slowly rises while circling above you, instead of a dark screen.
- When a medic is on the way, the camera follows and circles the medic at a lower height so you can watch them come to you.
- If the medic is lost or goes down, the camera returns to you.
- Night vision is used on the wounded camera at night.
