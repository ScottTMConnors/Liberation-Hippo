# 1.1.12

## ⚙️ Settings
- The Friendly-fire enforcement and Civilian casualty penalty settings are back, both off by default.
- The objective panel now fits ultrawide screens.

## 🌐 Multiplayer
- AI groups run by headless clients now follow battlegroup orders correctly.
- R3F heliport drops and rappelling work properly in multiplayer.
- Commander and moderator lists now come from the server, so a server's own lists reach every player.

## 🔧 Fixes
- Deleting a sector commander now counts as eliminating it.
- Manned trench guns are no longer cleaned up.
- Rejoining during a respawn cooldown no longer kicks you.
- Call Medic keeps the replacement medic, and the revive menu reopens after Esc.
- Recruited units get their proper loadouts, and the sell shop sells the right items.
- Side missions and wildlife keep running on an empty server.
- Many smaller script errors fixed after a full review.
