# Hippos Conquest credits

Hippos Conquest is maintained by **TheHungryHippo** (ScottTMConnors / STC).
The Hippo fork adds its gameplay rules, configurable AI warfare, faction catalog
tools, presentation, and integration changes on top of Liberation RX.

Based on **Liberation RX**, developed and maintained by **pSiKO** and its
contributors, and the original Liberation work by **Zbug** and contributors.
The inherited project also credits AgentRev, Larrow, GREUH, and the authors of
its included addons and scripts. Their existing source and in-game notices are
retained; see those components for their individual credits.

This fork preserves the project's [GNU GPL version 3 license](COPYING.md).
Fork maintenance and Hippo changes are credited separately from inherited work.
